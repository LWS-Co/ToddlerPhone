# ToddlerPhone for iOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS version of ToddlerPhone published by LWS-Co. It does not describe the Android version, whose permissions and services may differ. See the [Android privacy policy](https://github.com/LWS-Co/ToddlerPhone/blob/main/privacy.md) for that app.

## Information stored on your device

ToddlerPhone does not require an account. When a parent or guardian configures the app, it stores family contact names, phone numbers, selected photos or emoji, calling preferences, the optional parent PIN, and other app settings in the app's local iOS preferences. Voice recordings are stored in the app's private Application Support area. Photos and recordings are used by the app and are not uploaded to servers operated by LWS-Co.

The app does not access the iOS Contacts database. Contact details are entered in ToddlerPhone. The app asks for microphone access only when you start a voice recording. You can choose a photo or take one for a contact; iOS presents the system photo picker or camera permission prompt when needed.

Local app data may be included in device backups according to your iOS backup settings. You can remove contacts and recordings in the app. Removing ToddlerPhone deletes its local app data from the device, but data retained in a device backup may remain according to your backup settings.

## Calls and purchases

If Live Call is enabled, ToddlerPhone asks iOS to open the Phone app with the selected contact's phone number. iOS and your carrier handle the call. ToddlerPhone does not monitor call state or learn whether the call connected.

The Family Directory Pro purchase uses Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes the transaction under Apple's privacy policies. ToddlerPhone receives the purchase entitlement status and does not receive payment card details. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Sharing, advertising, and analytics

LWS-Co does not receive the contact details, photos, settings, or recordings stored by ToddlerPhone. The iOS app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The app has no LWS-Co-operated account or content server.

When you use a live call or make an in-app purchase, the relevant information is handled by iOS, your carrier, or Apple as described above and under their respective policies.

## Children and parents

ToddlerPhone is designed for family use. A parent or guardian should configure the family directory, choose what contact details and photos to add, and manage recordings and settings. The app does not send a child's profile information to LWS-Co.

The optional parent PIN protects access to app settings; it is not a device passcode and does not encrypt app data. Use the iOS device passcode to protect information stored on the device.
