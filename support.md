# ToddlerPhone Support

For help with ToddlerPhone for iOS, email [contact.lws.co@gmail.com](mailto:contact.lws.co@gmail.com).

To help us investigate, include your iPhone model, iOS version, ToddlerPhone version, and a description of what happened. Do not email a child's name, phone number, photo, voice recording, Apple ID, payment details, or password.

If Family Directory Pro does not appear unlocked after purchase, open Settings in ToddlerPhone and choose **Restore Purchases**. Apple processes App Store payments and refunds; visit [Apple's billing support](https://support.apple.com/billing) for billing help.

Read the [ToddlerPhone for iOS Privacy Policy](https://github.com/LWS-Co/ToddlerPhone/blob/main/privacy-ios.md).
