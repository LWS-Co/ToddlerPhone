# SafePass for iOS Support

For help with SafePass for iOS, email [contact.lws.co@gmail.com](mailto:contact.lws.co@gmail.com).

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, SafePass version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not email proof images or PDFs, passwords, passcodes, Apple Account credentials, or payment details.

## Common questions

**I can't unlock the library.** SafePass uses Face ID or your device passcode. Confirm that a device passcode or biometric authentication is configured in Settings.

**I can't add another proof.** The free library allows one proof total. The one-time Unlimited Proofs purchase raises that limit. In SafePass, choose **Unlock Unlimited** and use **Restore Purchase** if you already bought it with your Apple Account.

**Why can't I start a handoff?** iOS Guided Access must be enabled and active before SafePass shows proofs to a recipient. SafePass cannot prevent screenshots or independently lock the iPhone to the app.

**I need help with a charge or refund.** App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit [Apple Support for billing](https://support.apple.com/billing).

SafePass stores proofs locally on your device and excludes its library from device backups. Deleting the app may remove locally stored proofs. Keep another copy of documents you cannot afford to lose.

See the [SafePass for iOS Privacy Policy](privacy-safepass-ios.md).
