# SafePass for iOS Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

This policy describes SafePass for iOS. It is separate from any Android SafePass policy.

## Information handled on your device

SafePass has no account system or developer-operated server. Proof images and PDFs you import or capture, their folder names, and related library information are stored in the app's local storage. SafePass does not upload those proofs to LWS. The app requests access to the camera only when you choose to take a photo. Photos and Files are selected through Apple's system pickers; SafePass imports only the items you select.

The app protects its local files using iOS Data Protection and excludes its library from device backups. The library remains on the device until you delete the relevant folder or proof, or remove the app. Keep another copy of important documents.

## Authentication

SafePass asks iOS to authenticate the owner with Face ID or the device passcode. Authentication is performed by iOS. SafePass does not receive or store Face ID templates or other biometric data.

## Purchases

The optional Unlimited Proofs unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple using StoreKit. SafePass does not receive or store payment card details. Apple handles payment and purchase information under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). SafePass uses the purchase entitlement to enable unlimited proofs and provides a Restore Purchase action.

## Analytics, advertising, and sharing

SafePass for iOS does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not use a developer-operated service to track you. Proof files are not shared with LWS. If you choose to contact support, LWS receives the email address and message you send and uses them to respond to your request. Do not include proof files or sensitive credentials in support email.

## Your choices

You can delete proofs and folders within SafePass. You can deny camera access in iOS Settings; importing existing items remains available through the system pickers. You can request help or ask a privacy question at [contact.lws.co@gmail.com](mailto:contact.lws.co@gmail.com).

## Changes

If SafePass's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when the change takes effect. The current version is available at this URL.
